💡 Environmental Insights

🌱 Tips for a Greener Lifestyle

  • Use public transport, walk or cycle for short distances.
  • Switch off lights and appliances when not in use.
  • Carry a reusable water bottle and shopping bag.
  • Plant trees and take care of green spaces.
  • Save water by fixing leaks and using it carefully.
  • Separate dry and wet waste and recycle whenever possible.
  • Choose energy-efficient appliances.

🌍 Why Small Actions Matter

Every eco-friendly action helps reduce waste, conserve natural resources and protect the environment. By making small changes in our daily lives, we can contribute to a healthier and greener planet.
