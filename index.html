    💡 Environmental Insights

    Small changes can make a big difference to our planet.

    🚶 Walk or Cycle: Choose walking or cycling for short journeys instead of using a car.
    💡 Save Electricity: Switch off lights and appliances when they are not needed.
    ♻️ Recycle Waste: Separate dry and wet waste to support recycling.
    🧴 Use Reusable Items: Carry a reusable water bottle and shopping bag.
    💧 Save Water: Turn off taps when not in use and avoid wasting water.
    🌳 Plant Trees: Plant trees and take care of green spaces.
    🌞 Use Natural Light: Make use of daylight whenever possible.
    🌍 Spread Awareness: Encourage friends and family to follow eco-friendly habits.
